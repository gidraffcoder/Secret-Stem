Keep them fresh

Look after them well and our mums and alstroemeria arrangements last up to 21 days.

Roses5–7 days
Lilies10–14 days
Alstroemeriaup to 18 days
Mumsup to 21 days

  • Trim

    2 cm off each stem at an angle as soon as they arrive.

  • Fresh water

    Every two days. Rinse the vase and keep leaves out of the water.

  • Find a cool spot

    Away from direct sun, and away from the fruit bowl.

  • Water bouquets

    They come with their own reservoir. Top it up and don't untie it.
